StoryBox Melody Recorder

Play a melody on the FM-1 (or any MIDI keyboard) along with a click. Copy it as text for Claude, or download it as a MIDI file.

  1. Plug the FM-1 into this computer with USB and open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. On the FM-1's System page, turn MIDI out on. Press EDIT for lead mode and press PLAY so the music rests. (Its other parts also send MIDI, so recording only the lead's channel keeps them out.)
  3. Press Connect, set the tempo, then Record. You get one bar of clicks first, then play. (A pickup played in that bar is kept.) Press Stop when you're done.
